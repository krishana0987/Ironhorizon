Privacy Policy

IRON HORIZON

A 2D offline military action shooter · Package name com.krishanagames.ironhorizon · Last updated 14 October 2026

Short version: IRON HORIZON does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in and no analytics. The game keeps only your mission progress on your own device, and it never sends that anywhere.

1. What we do not collect

IRON HORIZON has no server and no developer-operated backend. The app itself makes no network requests of any kind. Specifically, we do not collect or process:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account identifier;
  • your location, contacts, photos, files, camera or microphone;
  • device identifiers, advertising identifiers or a fingerprint of your phone;
  • gameplay statistics, session logs, crash reports or analytics events;
  • anything from a third-party tracking or advertising SDK — the game contains no advertising SDKs and no tracking libraries.

2. What is stored on your device

The game saves two small records in the app's private on-device storage (localStorage), which belongs to the app sandbox on your phone:

RecordWhat it holdsWhy it exists
ironhorizon.progress.v1 Which missions you have unlocked and cleared, your star rating per mission and the highest mission you reached. So the campaign continues where you left off.
ironhorizon.entitlement.v1 A single yes/no copy of Google Play's last answer about whether this device owns the one-time full-campaign unlock. So a purchased campaign stays playable when the device is offline.

Neither record contains your name, an identifier, or any payment detail. They leave the device only through a normal Android backup if you have enabled it, which is Google's feature and is governed by Google's own privacy terms.

3. Why the app needs an internet permission

IRON HORIZON is playable offline. The INTERNET permission exists for one purpose only: to let Google Play Billing reach the Play Store so you can buy the full-campaign unlock and so the app can ask Play whether you already own it. The game never uploads your progress and never downloads content.

4. Purchases and Google Play Billing

All payments are handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google is the merchant of record and processes the transaction, receipts, refunds and tax documents.

  • The developer never sees your card details, payment instrument, or your Google account email address.
  • The app only ever receives a boolean answer from Play — this device owns the unlock, or it does not — plus the price of the product for display.
  • Your purchase history is data that Google holds about you, and it is covered by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy, not by this one. If you want that data deleted or exported, ask Google, for example through My Activity.
  • If you restore a purchase after reinstalling, the app re-asks Play on the device and caches the yes/no answer locally, as described in section 2.

5. Children

IRON HORIZON depicts military combat with stylised 2D gunfights and is not directed at children. It is intended for players aged 13 and above. Because the app collects nothing, no personal information is knowingly gathered from anyone of any age, and there is no advertising, no in-app chat and no link to social media.

6. Your rights, in practice

There is no personal data for us to access, correct, export or delete on request, because none is collected or held by the developer. Everything the app does store is yours and you can remove it at any time without asking us:

  • clear the game's data in Android Settings → Apps → IRON HORIZON → Storage → Clear data, or
  • uninstall the app. Both delete the two records described above immediately.
  • turning off Android's system backup stops the records from ever leaving the device.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of IRON HORIZON ever collects data, this page will be updated and dated before that version is released, and the game will not ship the change silently. Version 1.0 of the game, described above, collects nothing.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the developer contact details published on the IRON HORIZON page in Google Play (the Developer contact email and website shown in the "About" section of the store listing).

This policy applies to the IRON HORIZON Android application only. It does not apply to Google Play, to Google Play Billing, or to any other app you install on your device.
